FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What information do you need for a quotation?

Valve type, size, pressure class, body/trim material, end connection, operating media and temperature, quantity and delivery location. A datasheet is ideal.

What is your typical lead time?

Standard sizes in carbon and stainless steel ship from stock within 1–2 weeks. Made-to-order and special alloys typically take 8–14 weeks.

Do you provide third-party inspection?

Yes. We regularly work with TÜV, Bureau Veritas, Lloyd's, DNV and customer-nominated inspectors, with full MTC 3.1 / 3.2 documentation.

Can you supply actuated valves?

Yes. We supply complete packages with pneumatic, electric or hydraulic actuators, positioners, limit switches and solenoid valves, fully tested together.
